About Us

We're building the future of analytics.

Nexora was born from a simple idea: every team deserves access to powerful, AI-driven insights — without needing a data science degree.

Our Story

From a weekend hack to 10,000+ teams.

In 2021, three engineers frustrated with slow, clunky BI tools decided to build something better. What started as a weekend project quickly became the analytics platform modern teams actually enjoy using.

Today, Nexora powers decision-making at startups, scale-ups, and Fortune 500 companies across 40+ countries — processing billions of data points every single day.
